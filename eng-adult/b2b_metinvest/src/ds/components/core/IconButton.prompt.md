Circular icon-only button for toolbars, player controls, close affordances and carousel arrows.

```jsx
<IconButton label="Наступний слайд" variant="secondary"><i data-lucide="chevron-right"></i></IconButton>
```

Variants `primary` / `secondary` / `ghost`; sizes 32/40/48px. Icons come from Lucide at 1.75px stroke (see ICONOGRAPHY in readme.md).
