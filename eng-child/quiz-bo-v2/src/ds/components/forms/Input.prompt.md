Text field for sign-up, trial-lesson booking and in-app settings.

```jsx
<Input label="Ваш телефон" type="tel" required placeholder="+380" hint="Ми надішлемо посилання на урок"/>
```

Labels are 14px SemiBold above the field; hints and errors are 12px Medium below. Focus shows a 3px `--focus-ring` in 32% orange.
